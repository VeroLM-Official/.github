# 🚀 VeroLM

**Code, papers, and experiments behind the VeroLM models.**

VeroLM is an independent AI lab training language models at every scale, from tiny edge-friendly models to large ones. Our models and datasets live on Hugging Face and are mostly open source. This is where the code, research, and write-ups behind them live.

👉 **Models and datasets:** [huggingface.co/VeroLM](https://huggingface.co/VeroLM)

## 📦 What you'll find here

- **Code:** training, inference, and tooling for our models, including runners that work directly in the browser
- **Papers and write-ups:** notes, reports, and papers about what we build and why it works (or doesn't)
- **Experiments:** new and rarely explored ideas in architecture, training, and optimization, shared as we go

## 💡 What we care about

- **Quality first.** We build models that genuinely perform, not just models that exist.
- **Speed and efficiency.** Fast, optimized, and able to run on the hardware people actually have.
- **Open by default.** Most of what we make is open source, and we share what we learn.
